# War Related

## Russian Ukranine Monitor Map

The Centre for Information Resilience, alongside Bellingcat and GeoConfirmed, have been collecting, verifying and sharing geolocated data on Russian military activities in and around Ukraine on an online map entitled “Russia-Ukraine Monitor Map” since January 2022. As of December 2022, the map has been moved to EyesOnRussia.org.&#x20;
